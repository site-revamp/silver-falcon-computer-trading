# Silver Falcon Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Silver Falcon Computer Trading LLC is a Dubai-based B2B technology solutions provider offering IT infrastructure, networking, security systems, technical support, and POS products for restaurants, cafés, retailers, and other businesses.

The redesigned homepage focuses on presenting the company's solutions through three clear customer pathways: IT Infrastructure & Support, Security & Access Systems, and POS & Retail Technology. The overall direction prioritises consistent brand identity, easier service discovery, and a more effective product enquiry journey.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Consistent Brand Identity and Business Information
- Standardised the presentation of the Silver Falcon company name and brand identity.
- Addressed inconsistent naming, including references to Mignet and Silver Falkon.
- Improved the consistency of business information and contact details throughout the homepage.
- Prioritised the current contact-page address over outdated address references, pending final business verification.

### 2. Clearer Homepage Structure for Different Customer Groups
- Organised the homepage around three prominent service pathways:
  - **IT Infrastructure & Support:** Server installation, networking, cabling, and IT maintenance.
  - **Security & Access Systems:** CCTV, surveillance, and access control solutions.
  - **POS & Retail Technology:** Restaurant, café, and retail POS equipment.
- Made the different service categories easier to identify.
- Helped visitors navigate towards solutions relevant to their business requirements.

### 3. Improved POS Product Discovery and Enquiry Journey
- Created a more structured presentation of POS products and equipment.
- Improved product category visibility and the presentation of relevant product information.
- Introduced clearer pathways for exploring products and requesting quotations.
- Made it easier for restaurant, café, and retail customers to identify relevant POS solutions and take the next step.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Strengthened brand consistency and established a clearer visual hierarchy across the homepage.
- **Layout:** Organised business solutions into three distinct customer-focused sections.
- **User experience:** Simplified navigation between IT services, security solutions, and POS products.
- **Consistency:** Improved the presentation of company identity, service information, and business contact details.
- **Product presentation:** Gave POS equipment and product categories a clearer role in the homepage structure.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a more organised, professional business technology website focused on service discovery and product enquiries.

## Project Objective

The redesign addresses the three critical issues identified during the original website research: inconsistent brand identity and business information, unclear customer navigation, and weak POS product discovery. The overall objective is to position Silver Falcon as a unified business technology provider while making its services and products easier to explore and enquire about.
